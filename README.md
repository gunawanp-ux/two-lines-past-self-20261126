# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca): two lines — a warning to my past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered.

Running `main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (264 characters, within the 1–280 gate):

> When the recruiter calls at 9:03 on Tuesday and offers you the safe job, don't say yes -- say 'let me think about it.'
> That one pause would have kept the door open long enough for the risky job to call back the next morning, and that job is the one that made you.
